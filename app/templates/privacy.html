YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

A plain-language summary for this proof of concept.

What Saor stores

Your email and password hash, account status, and business preparation details you enter. That can include your business idea, town and county, payment option, claim start date, yes/no pre-check answers, business projections, notes, uploads and mentor email you choose to share with.

What Saor does not ask you for

Do not enter health details, ID or PPS numbers, passport information, bank details, street address, phone number, nationality, family details or account logins. Do not upload ID documents.

Where it stays

This proof of concept stores data in its local application database and uploads folder. Demo data is fictional. The app does not send real invitation email, submit forms, take payments or include analytics.

Your choices

You can export your application data as JSON, remove mentor access, or delete your account and saved work from your applicant workspace.

This notice is a draft for review by the project owner. Independent preparation tool; results are indicative.
